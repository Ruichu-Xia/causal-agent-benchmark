Held-out test task iid_test-88 · the question
"Is severe_poverty statistically associated with accident after conditioning on change and additional_expenses, in the population?"
Columns: accident, complications, additional_expenses, disability, change, severe_poverty. Answer Yes or No.
Both shortcuts point to the wrong answer, No: the plain correlation is near zero, and neither name plausibly causes the other.
Hidden · why the label is Yes
conditioned onpath that conditioning opens
additional_expenses is a common effect of both. On their own the two are unrelated (r = 0.02, p = 0.39), but conditioning on a common effect links them (r = 0.13, p ≈ 1.5e−9).

Data + names
What the agent should do: analyze the rows, and use the names to choose among graphs the data cannot tell apart. 1.00 by construction.
Data only
A perfect analysis of the data that ignores the names. Where several graphs fit the data equally well, it guesses among them.
Names only
Answers from the links the names make plausible (CauseNet), never looking at the data.
Shortcut
The family's most tempting habit. On independence and adjustment it does no better than a constant answer; on structure and effect size it does, a known limitation.
Same answer
The best single answer given to every item: the chance level for that family.
